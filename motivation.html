1. Motivation & Research

The Why: Project Motivation

Through questionnaire surveys of tourists and local residents, we found that visitors often lack clear guidance on the visiting process at Chang Gate. Most tourists only regard historical sites as a casual walking experience without actively engaging with the related history and culture. After the visit, they still know little about the site's cultural background and historical stories, which is not conducive to cultural communication and inheritance.

Interviews with local residents also show that young residents generally have only shallow knowledge of the history and culture of their community, further weakening the protection and spread of local heritage. Therefore, this project aims to help visitors learn about historical culture in a relaxed and interesting way during sightseeing. In addition, for visitors who prefer planned and structured tours, the project will provide AR-based navigation to help them find clear visiting routes and directions, effectively improving the overall touring experience.

Stakeholders

Our project serves two primary stakeholder groups: Visitors (primary users) who explore cultural heritage sites, and Residents (secondary users) who live near heritage locations and care about cultural preservation.

👤 Primary: Visitors

Tourists and travelers who visit Chang Gate for leisure, education, or cultural exploration.

Visitor Profile

  • Age: 18-45 years old
  • Tech Savvy: Comfortable with smartphone apps
  • Goals: Learn history, take photos, share experiences
  • Pain Points: Limited historical info, poor navigation, shallow engagement

🏠 Secondary: Residents

Local community members, especially young residents, who have connection to the heritage site.

Resident Profile

  • Age: 16-35 years old
  • Connection: Live near Chang Gate area
  • Goals: Learn about heritage, preserve culture, engage community
  • Pain Points: Limited knowledge of local history, lack of engagement tools

Research Gap

The key gap identified in current AR heritage applications is the lack of seamless web-based experiences that combine playful design, cultural storytelling, and onsite navigation specifically for Asian heritage sites. Most existing solutions either require app installation, lack gamification, or fail to provide meaningful cultural narratives. Our web-based AR approach addresses these gaps by offering an all-in-one solution with engaging gameplay, rich cultural content, and intuitive navigation support.
