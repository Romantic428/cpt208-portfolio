Key Pain Points Identified

🗺️
Route Confusion

Visitors don't know the best path to explore Chang Gate, leading to inefficient navigation and missed attractions.

📜
Lack of Cultural Context

Tourists lack understanding of the historical significance and cultural stories behind the heritage site.

System Requirements List

🎙️

Feature 1: Location-Based Audio Guide

When visitors approach a heritage site (within 50-meter radius), the system automatically detects their location and displays relevant historical information along with audio narration. This provides contextual cultural knowledge without requiring manual searching.

  • Auto-detect proximity to heritage points
  • Display text descriptions of the site
  • Play audio narration on demand
  • Support multiple languages
🧭

Feature 2: AR Navigation Guidance

The system provides visual navigation arrows overlaid on the camera view, guiding visitors along recommended routes. AR markers appear in the real-world view to indicate direction and distance to the next point of interest.

  • AR arrow overlay on camera feed
  • Real-time distance indicator
  • Multiple route options (scenic, quick, comprehensive)
  • Turn-by-turn visual guidance
🥚

Feature 3: Easter Egg Memory Sharing

A playful feature allowing visitors to capture and share memories. Tourists can take photos at checkpoints, while local residents can contribute personal stories and memories associated with the heritage site, creating a collective cultural archive.

  • Photo capture at designated checkpoints
  • Memory upload for residents (text/stories)
  • Local storage of check-in history
  • Gallery view of captured moments

Functional Requirements
