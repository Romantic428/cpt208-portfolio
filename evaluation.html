Iterative Improvements

Based on user feedback, several improvements were made between the alpha and beta versions:

Before (Alpha)

  • 30-second GPS timeout
  • No fallback position
  • Fixed AR marker brightness

After (Beta)

  • 10-second GPS timeout
  • Virtual coordinate fallback
  • Auto-adjusting marker brightness

Challenges & Solutions

GPS Timeout

Solution: Implemented virtual coordinate fallback with 10-second timeout

AR Rendering

Solution: Adjusted elevation parameters in A-Frame configuration

Cross-Browser Compatibility

Solution: Added feature detection for camera and geolocation APIs

Social & Ethical Impact Reflection

Digital Divide Considerations

While this AR tour provides an innovative way to engage with cultural heritage, it relies on smartphones with GPS and camera capabilities. This creates a potential digital divide, excluding visitors who may not have access to such devices. To address this, future iterations could explore low-tech alternatives or partner with local tourism offices to provide device rentals.

Privacy Concerns

The application collects location data and user-generated content (photos). While currently stored locally on the user's device, any future cloud integration must prioritize data privacy. Users should have full control over their data, including the ability to delete photos and location history at any time.

Cultural Preservation vs. Commercialization

There is a delicate balance between making cultural heritage accessible and avoiding over-commercialization. The AR tour should enhance, not replace, the authentic experience of visiting historical sites. Care must be taken to ensure that the technology serves as a complement to, rather than a distraction from, the cultural significance of Chang Gate and its surroundings.

Inclusivity and Accessibility

The current implementation relies heavily on visual and auditory elements, which may not be accessible to users with visual or hearing impairments. Future development should incorporate accessibility features such as text-to-speech, high contrast modes, and alternative navigation methods for users with disabilities.

Community Engagement

By allowing local residents to share their stories and memories through the "Resident Stories" feature, the project fosters community engagement and strengthens the connection between residents and their cultural heritage. This participatory approach helps preserve living history that might otherwise be lost.

Conclusion

The Chang Gate AR Tour project successfully demonstrates the potential of web-based AR for cultural heritage tourism. By combining GPS-based positioning with interactive check-ins, we created an engaging experience that bridges historical knowledge with modern technology. Future improvements could include backend integration for social features, 3D historical models, and multi-language support.
