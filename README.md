# Chang Gate AR Tour - Portfolio

This portfolio showcases the Chang Gate AR Tour project, an innovative web-based augmented reality application designed to enhance cultural heritage tourism at Chang Gate, Suzhou.

## Project Overview

The Chang Gate AR Tour is a mobile web application that combines GPS-based positioning with augmented reality technology to provide visitors with an immersive historical exploration experience.

## Key Features

1. **Location-Based Audio Guide** - Automatic historical information and audio narration when approaching heritage sites
2. **AR Navigation Guidance** - Visual navigation arrows overlaid on camera view for route guidance
3. **Easter Egg Memory Sharing** - Check-in feature for tourists to capture photos and residents to share personal stories

## 1. Motivation & Research
Motivation:  
Through questionnaire surveys of tourists and local residents, we found that visitors often lack clear guidance on the visiting process at Chang Gate. Most tourists only regard historical sites as a casual walking experience without actively engaging with the related history and culture. After the visit, they still know little about the site’s cultural background and historical stories, which is not conducive to cultural communication and inheritance. Interviews with local residents also show that young residents generally have only shallow knowledge of the history and culture of their community, further weakening the protection and spread of local heritage. Therefore, this project aims to help visitors learn about historical culture in a relaxed and interesting way during sightseeing. In addition, for visitors who prefer planned and structured tours, the project will provide AR-based navigation to help them find clear visiting routes and directions, effectively improving the overall touring experience.
